04 / Before we start

Good questions.
Straight answers.

Do I need to know exactly what to ask for?

No. Describe the task, where it gets frustrating and what a better result would look like. I'll help narrow it down before proposing paid work. The initial fit discussion is free; detailed implementation and discovery are scoped separately.

Which tools and websites can you help with?

Typical requests involve spreadsheets, documents, shared files, recurring reports, small application workflows and existing brochure websites. Send the names of the tools you use. I'll confirm compatibility and whether I can deliver the task before accepting it. Not every platform or integration is a fit.

Do I have to buy new software or use AI?

No. The starting point is the tools you already have. If a paid tool or AI service would help, I'll explain why, discuss the costs and data involved, and get your approval first. There is no requirement to use AI.

How are the business AI engagements different?

They address a broader workflow, pilot or portfolio with your business and technical teams. Each is separately scoped and quoted, with named owners, acceptance criteria and an agreed time commitment. They are not included in the Practical Fix, monthly small-business packages or their USD 75/hour extra-work rate. The initial fit discussion is free; a detailed sprint or pilot is paid work agreed in writing.

What happens if the work is bigger than expected?

The scope and acceptance criteria are agreed before payment. If a dependency or new requirement changes the job, I'll explain it and agree a revised approach with you before extra work. No automatic overages or surprise charges.

How do monthly billing and cancellation work?

The small-business monthly packages are billed in advance, with no minimum term. Cancel by email before your next renewal to stop the next billing period. Your start date, renewal date and scope are confirmed in the written service agreement. Unused hours expire at the end of each period; they do not roll over. Separately quoted AI engagements have their own agreed payment, timing and cancellation terms, with no automatic subscription.

Are taxes, software and hosting included?

Prices are in US dollars. Any applicable taxes are identified in your written quote before you commit. Third-party software, licences, domains, hosting and platform charges are separate and require your approval. Your accounts remain in your name.

Is this emergency IT support or full website management?

No. This is planned, bounded business support, not a 24/7 helpdesk, cybersecurity service or uptime guarantee. Website care covers small updates and agreed checks, not security monitoring, emergency recovery or full operational responsibility. Larger builds, complex migrations and business-critical systems need a separate assessment.

Does enquiring start a subscription?

No. An enquiry is just a conversation. There is no payment, account creation or subscription on this page. We'll agree the work, availability, price and service terms in writing before you decide.
