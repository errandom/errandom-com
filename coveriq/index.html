From scattered policies to a reviewable record

Organise the documents

Bring together readable PDFs and text documents for a policy. Review suggested details before saving; scanned documents need a separate text or OCR copy.

Keep evidence with the answer

Record inclusions, exclusions, limits and deductibles with document and page quotations. Missing or unresolved evidence stays unknown, not automatically covered.

Review the next deadline

Confirm renewal and insurer-receipt dates from source documents. Use in-app reminders and private calendar exports; check your calendar's alert settings yourself.

What this looks like in practice

A fictional example, not a customer result.

You have a household policy, its general conditions and a later amendment. Keep all three together, quote the clause you rely on and record what still needs clarification. Before considering cancellation, check the contract's notice rules and what recorded protection you would lose.

A draft or sent letter does not mean the insurer accepted a cancellation. CoverIQ tracks sending, evidence of receipt and evidence of acceptance separately. Ordinary cancellation drafts are Swiss-first and exclude health/life policies and special grounds; nothing is sent automatically.

Private documents stay out of public search

Documents are encrypted on your device with a separate vault passphrase. Browser-local storage and optional encrypted cloud storage are distinct choices; hosted accounts sit behind the preview gate.

Keep your passphrase and an encrypted backup safe: account password recovery does not recover the vault passphrase. Review the current privacy, terms and security notices in the preview before creating an account or importing documents.

An organiser, not an insurance adviser

CoverIQ is not automatic AI contract interpretation, insurer approval or a guarantee of cover. Confirm policy details and interpretations yourself, and ask your insurer or a qualified adviser about consequential decisions.

There is no automatic insurer switching, claim submission or letter sending. Email reminders are not enabled in the current preview. A calendar export is a snapshot: replace it when verified dates change.
