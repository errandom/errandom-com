QMT by errandom

Practice, game and field management for American football and flag football clubs.

Less coordination overhead. More time on the field. Bring teams, training sessions, games, facilities and equipment into one shared schedule.

Single-club pilot: access for club staff.

QMT is available for the Zurich Renegades, with an authenticated operations office for authorized staff. Use the application link below; there is no public registration.

Service access is budget-controlled. If the application is temporarily unavailable, including during a budget pause, this independent page remains available. Contact errandom about access. This page is not a real-time availability monitor.

Start focused. Earn the right to expand.

The first release is for one configured club, not a public multi-club service. Other field sports are a possible future direction, not a claim of current support.

Core scheduling works without AI or external integrations; both are disabled in this pilot. There is no public signup or paid subscription offer at this stage.
