About

errandom is the umbrella brand of Kenneth Fischer — a builder focused on tools and services that quietly remove friction from everyday life and work.

The thread running through every venture is judgment over automation: technology should calibrate to your priorities, respect your privacy by design, and earn trust before it acts. Whether it's an AI Chief of Staff or a spotless home, the goal is the same — hand people back their time and attention.

Some ventures are software, some are hands-on services, and more are on the way. errandom is where they all live.
